## 1 · FACEBOOK

**Setup**
- **Page name:** `Basically Elite`
- **Username:** `@basicallyelite` → `facebook.com/basicallyelite`
- **Category (up to 3):** **Digital creator** → **Sports** → **Just for Fun** *(fallback primary if unavailable: Content creator / Personal blog)*
- **Button:** **Follow** (not Message — you're a creator, not a service)
- **Intro / About:**
  > `Faceless running page for everyone from never-started to basically-Kipchoge. The science is fake — the pain is real. New study every day. Tell me which level you are 👇`

**Facebook SEO / discovery**
- Put searchable words in the **About** and **Bio** fields ("running", "running memes", "runner humor") — FB search indexes these, not the page name.
- **Reels is the reach engine on FB right now** — post there, not just the feed. FB actively pushes Reels + pays Reels bonuses in some regions (money you want).
- FB rewards **longer captions + a question** (it drives comments, which FB weights heavily). End every post with a question.
- **Share each Reel into 2–3 relevant running Facebook Groups** (huge untapped reach). Don't spam — post as value/humor.

**Best audio on Facebook**
- FB Reels has its **own** music/trending panel (separate from IG). Use the **"Trending" audio tab inside FB Reels** — FB trends lag IG/TikTok by ~1–2 weeks, so sounds that already peaked on TikTok often work great here.
- Safe evergreen: emotional electronic (Fred again.. type) + cinematic swells. (See §4.)

**Setup**

- **Handle:** `@basicallyelite`

- **Link:** your Linktree.

**Instagram SEO / discovery**
- IG is now a **search engine** — it indexes your **Name field, bio, captions, and on-screen text**. Put target keywords ("running", "zone 2", "marathon") in the caption's **first line** and in the **on-screen text** (it gets read).
- **Reels only** for growth. Feed posts/carousels are for retention, not reach.
- **Alt text:** add keyworded alt text to every post (Advanced settings → Alt text).
- **Hashtags:** 5–10, mixed (see §6). Put them in the caption, not the first comment.
- **Trial Reels:** IG lets you push a Reel only to non-followers first to test — use it on risky jokes.
- **Collab (invite as co-author):** team up with other running/meme pages → your Reel posts to both audiences.

**Best audio on Instagram**
- Use audio marked with the little **↑ "trending" arrow** in the Reels audio search — that's IG telling you it's rising. Prioritize sounds **under ~50k uses** (room to ride the wave) over ones with millions (already saturated).
- **Save sounds** you like (bookmark them) so you can batch-film to them later.
- Add your own **"original audio"** occasionally — if a Reel pops, others reuse *your* sound and credit links back to you.

## 3 · TIKTOK

**Setup**
- **Handle:** `@basicallyelite`
- **★ Name field:** `Basically Elite | Running` (searchable — same trick as IG)
- **Bio (80 chars — short!):**
  > `Peer-reviewed cope for runners. Zone 2 forever. New study daily 👇`
- **Link in bio** unlocks at 1k followers; until then, drive to IG in the bio text.

**TikTok SEO (TikTok is the #1 search engine for Gen Z — this matters most here)**
- **Say the keyword out loud, show it as on-screen text, AND type it in the caption.** TikTok transcribes audio + reads text + reads captions to rank you in search. Triple it.
- First caption line = a real search phrase ("marathon training", "how to run", "zone 2 cardio") — then subvert it.
- **3–5 hashtags**, keyword-style (see §6). More keyword than hashtag.
- **Playlists:** group your posts into named playlists ("Fake Studies", "Cope Science") — boosts watch-time + searchability.
- **Reply to comments with a video** — free content + the algorithm loves it.
- **Stitch/Duet** running takes and react in your deadpan voice.

**Best audio on TikTok**
- Open the sound library → **filter by "Trending"** and sort. Grab **rising** sounds (added in the last ~7–14 days), not ones already on a million videos.
- TikTok explicitly boosts videos using **trending sounds** — this is the single biggest free reach lever on the platform.
- **Favorite** trending sounds as you scroll (long-press → Add to Favorites) so you have a stash to film to.

## 4 · THE SONG / AUDIO STRATEGY (what actually goes viral)

**Principle:** trending audio beats owned audio for reach, and **the funniest choice is contrast** — sincere, epic, or sad music under a stupid claim. That gap *is* the joke (it's why the espresso meme used a moody Fred again.. track).

⚠️ **Trends move weekly and I can't see this week's chart** — so use the in-app "trending" panels above to grab *today's* hot sound. But these four **evergreen "sound jobs"** always work for this brand — search these vibes and you'll never be stuck:

| The job | Vibe / what to search | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| 🎧 **Deadpan montage** *(your core sound)* | Emotional electronic — **Fred again..**, slowed + reverb pop, melodic house, "sad boy" indie | Aesthetic neck-down running shots + dry cope text |
| 🔥 **Locked-in grind** | **Phonk** (search "phonk", Kordhell / Dxrk ダーク vibe), hard beat-switch | Hard-effort shots, 5am, sprint finishes |
| 🎬 **Contrast comedy** | Epic cinematic swells — **Hans Zimmer "Time" / Interstellar**-type, orchestral builds | Amplify a dumb flex ("basically Kipchoge") — grandeur over nonsense |
| 😂 **Meme sound of the week** | Whatever specific spoken/viral sound is trending *right now* (the ↑ arrow) | Ride a viral format for a reach spike |

**Rule of thumb:** ~70% of posts on the **meme sound of the week** (for reach) + ~30% on your **deadpan montage** core sound (for brand identity). Cut the beat drop to land exactly on your punchline text.

## 8 · CROSS-POST WORKFLOW (film once → 3 platforms)

1. **Film** the neck-down clip clean (no captions burned in yet, no watermark).
2. **TikTok first** — add on-screen text + trending TikTok sound natively. Post.
3. **Re-upload the clean file to IG Reels** — add IG's trending audio + text natively (don't repost the TikTok export).
4. **Re-upload clean to FB Reels** — FB audio + a longer caption with a question.
5. **Retune the caption per platform** (TikTok short + slang, IG medium, FB longer + question).
