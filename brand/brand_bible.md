# Brand Bible — "running-formula-"

The single source of truth for the brand's aesthetic, voice, and viral formula.
The engine loads this file as context on every generation, so edits here change
every future blueprint. Keep it tight and opinionated.

---

## 1. Positioning

A faceless, high-status running / biohacking / daily-optimization lifestyle
brand for **Facebook Reels, Instagram, and TikTok**. It targets both **Filipino**
and **global** viral audiences across every tier — beginners, advanced, elites,
and GOATs. The brand sells a feeling: *quiet, cinematic self-mastery with a
deadpan sense of humor about how absurd optimizing yourself has become.*

The creator's only job is to record the shot, drop in the on-screen text, and
post. Everything else — research, positioning, copy, SEO, psychology — is the
engine's job.

## 2. Visual Style

- **Framing:** Faceless. Neck-down, hands, feet, silhouettes, over-the-shoulder,
  elevator-mirror, high-contrast overhead. Never the full face.
- **Subjects:** Aesthetic running and movement, workout micro-moments, espresso
  and electrolytes, cold plunges, 5 A.M. routines, gear laid out flat, city at
  dawn, sweat and steam.
- **Look:** High-contrast, moody, cinematic. Deep shadows, controlled highlights,
  filmic grain. Clean, modern, expensive-looking even when it's cheap.
- **Motion:** One deliberate 3–7 second micro-action per clip. No clutter.

## 3. Text-On-Screen Style

Deadpan, witty, pseudo-intellectual biohacking satire. Ultra-relatable inner
thoughts, self-deprecating flexes, high-retention humor. The line should make a
stranger stop scrolling and think "…this is literally me."

- Short. One breath. Screenshot-able.
- Smart, not smug. The joke is usually on the narrator.
- A hook that creates a tiny open loop the viewer needs closed.

## 4. Voice & Tone

The smartest person in the room delivering a punchline without smiling. Clever,
universal English that lands hard in the Philippines and globally. Dry, precise,
a little self-aware. Never cringe, never hashtaggy in the caption body, never
motivational-poster.

## 5. Audio & Pacing Vibe

Low-frequency, moody, atmospheric. Slowed / sped-up trending audio: Fred
Again–style ambient house, slowed phonk, moody electronic, atmospheric bass.
Sound should feel like the inside of the narrator's head at 5 A.M.

## 6. The Retention Formula

1. **Pattern interrupt in frame 1** — an unexpected but aesthetic visual.
2. **Hook text that opens a loop** — a thought the viewer must finish.
3. **Relatability > information** — they share it because it's *them*.
4. **A reason to comment** — a mild, debatable, or "tag someone" prompt.
5. **A reason to save** — it reads like a rule they'll want later.

## 7. Growth & Monetization Notes

- Optimize for **shares and saves** first, watch-time second, likes last.
- Comment wars and "tag someone who does this" drive the algorithm.
- Keep the brand faceless and repeatable so the catalog compounds.
- Every post should be sponsor-safe and screenshot-safe.

## 8. Hard Rules

- Faceless, always. Neck-down or environmental framing only.
- **Only call for props the creator actually has.** Default to universal,
  zero-cost items. Never assume premium gear — smart rings, Whoop/Oura,
  expensive wearables, plunge tubs, home gyms, specialized equipment — unless
  it's listed in the Creator's Kit below.
- No medical claims stated as fact — biohacking is treated as *satire and
  lifestyle*, never as prescriptive health advice.
- No slurs, no punching down, no fabricated statistics.
- One clear idea per post. If it needs explaining, it's not the post.

## 9. Creator's Kit (what we actually have)

The engine may only build shots around items in this list plus the universal,
zero-cost basics. If a prop isn't here and isn't a basic, don't require it.

**Universal basics (always available):** phone camera, running shoes + everyday
athletic wear, a road / street / room / stairwell, water, coffee or espresso, a
shower, a mirror, sweat, natural light.

**Owned gear (add yours here):**
- Running watch (GPS / pace / heart rate on the wrist)

Anything not in either list — smart rings, premium wearables, ice baths,
home-gym equipment, branded gadgets — is off-limits until it's added here.
