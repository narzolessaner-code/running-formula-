## Step 2 — Upload lahat + ang napiling music file (kung hindi trending-audio route), paste this:

```
You are the senior video editor for "TAKBO LAB," a faceless running-science
channel from the Philippines. This is the channel INTRO video. I uploaded 2
clips (a running POV looking down at legs + road, and a front-view running
shot), a voiceover, and brand PNGs. Edit ONE vertical video (9:16,
1080x1920, ~26 seconds). The voiceover (vo_intro.mp3) is the master
timeline — sync every cut to it. Never show a face; crop or reframe any
frame that contains one (this footage is neck-down, keep it that way).

BRANDING: place watermark.png top-right at 8% frame width, 60% opacity, for
the ENTIRE video. End with endcard.png full screen for the final 1.5s.

=== TIMELINE (EDL) ===
0.0–2.0s — shot1_pov.mp4. Music near-silent here. On-screen text slams in
word by word, huge bold white condensed all-caps with hard black shadow:
"BUKAS NA ANG LAB." Deep bass hit on the last word.
2.0–10.0s — continue shot1_pov.mp4 (slow it slightly if needed to fill).
Music builds gradually. Callout at 4s, monospace: "WALANG MUKHA. PURO
DATOS." and at 8s: "RUNNING SCIENCE, TINEST SA SARILING BINTI".
10.0–12.0s — one quick whoosh + hard cut to shot2_front.mp4.
12.0–18.0s — shot2_front.mp4. Music keeps building. Callout at 14s:
"ZONE 2 · CARBON · INIT · TULOG · CADENCE".
18.0–26.0s — continue shot2_front.mp4, its strongest strides. Place the
MUSIC BEAT DROP at exactly 18.0s. Callout at 20s: "BAGONG EXPERIMENT KADA
LINGGO", at 24s: "TAKBO LAB 'TO." Music ducks under the final voiceover
line, then endcard.

=== CAPTIONS ===
Auto-caption the entire Tagalog voiceover, word-by-word pop style, centered
lower third, white bold with black outline. Keep the Tagalog spelling
exactly as spoken; do not translate.

=== SOUND ===
Voiceover always on top and clear (music ducked -12dB under speech).
The first 2 seconds must be near-silent except the bass hit so the spoken
hook dominates. Build the music through the video and land the drop at 18s.
Add subtle SFX: soft foot-strike sounds on the running clips, one whoosh on
the 10s cut.

=== COLOR GRADE ("Manila Blue Hour") ===
Teal-blue shadows, warm orange highlights, crushed blacks, slightly lifted
contrast, ~15% film grain. Consistent across both clips.

=== EXPORT ===
9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30fps, high bitrate, all text inside the middle
80% of the frame.

If a clip is too short for its slot, slow it down (down to 0.85x) or loop
the tail — do not change the voiceover timing. If you cannot render video
directly, output the final timeline as a CapCut-ready cut list instead.
Show the final cut plus any spots where footage didn't cover the timeline.
```
