# 📱 EP001 FIELD SHOOT CARD — open this outside, nothing else

**⚙️ Phone setup (at home):** Vertical · 4K 60fps · wipe lens · tap-hold to lock focus/exposure · every shot 2–3 takes × 10s

**👕 Wear/bring:** running fit + watch · phone + gimbal · water (splash for sweat shots)

**🕐 Best time:** before sunrise or golden hour (streetlights or low sun)

---

## SHOTS (shooting order)

**A — WATCH, HIGH HR 🔴** — Sprint hard until watch reads 170+. Film wrist close-up mid-run, screen readable, slight shake OK. *30cm from wrist, 10s.*

**B — HANDS ON KNEES, GASPING 💦** — Right after the sprint. Side angle, chest-down only: hands on knees, chest heaving, sweat dripping. *Waist height, 1m away, very still, 15s.*

**C — SLOW RUN POV 👟** — Recover, then run genuinely slow. Phone at chest pointing down at your own legs. *Feet + road in frame, 15s.*

**D — WATCH, LOW HR ✅** — When watch reads ~135–145: wrist close-up mid-run, relaxed, then tilt up to relaxed shoulders (stop at neck). *30cm, smooth, 10s.*

**E — FOOT STRIKE MACRO 🎯** — Phone at ground level, side angle. Run past 3–4 times, shoe fills frame. Bonus: puddle or pour water on road.

**F — HERO SHOT 🌅** — Golden hour/streetlights. Run through frame 3–4 times at smooth pace, framed shoulders-down or full silhouette. Camera on gimbal or propped.

---

**❌ FACE RULE:** face enters frame = reshoot or angle down. Neck-down always.
**📵 NO GIMBAL?** Brace elbows against your sides (shots A/D), prop the phone on a bag/curb/shoe (shots B/E/F), and use slow-mo — 60fps slow-mo hides the shake (shots A/E).
**✅ DONE WHEN:** 6 shots × 2 good takes ≈ 20–25 min session.
