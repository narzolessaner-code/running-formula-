# EP002 — "Bakit Mas Mabagal Ka sa Pilipinas (At Bakit Advantage Mo 'Yun)" (Heat Adaptation)

## 🔬 1. Science / Experiment Anchor
- **Fact:** Heat-acclimation research (Lorenzo et al., 2010, trained cyclists) found ~10 days of training in heat improved VO2max by ~5% and time-trial performance — even when tested in COOL conditions. Mechanism: heat training expands blood plasma volume (commonly ~5–10%), so the heart pumps more oxygen-rich blood per beat. Sports scientists call heat "the poor man's altitude training."
- **Pinoy Hook:** Foreign elites pay thousands for heat chambers and sauna protocols to simulate what every Pinoy runner gets free at 38°C heat index. Your slow pace in Manila heat isn't weakness — it's a training stimulus others have to buy. Come December race season (or any cool-weather race), the heat-adapted Pinoy runner is supercharged.
