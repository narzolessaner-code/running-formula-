# 📱 EP002 FIELD SHOOT CARD — open this outside, nothing else

**⚙️ Phone setup (at home):** Vertical · 4K 60fps · wipe lens · tap-hold lock focus/exposure · every shot 2–3 takes × 10s
**👕 Wear/bring:** running fit + watch · phone + gimbal · 2 water bottles (1 to drink, 1 to pour) · small towel for the lens
**🕐 Best time:** THIS EPISODE NEEDS THE SUN — shoot 11 AM–2 PM for shots A–E, plus ONE early-morning shot (F). Two short sessions.

---

## SESSION 1 — NOON (the hotter the better)

**A — SWEAT DROP MACRO 💦** — Run 5–10 min first so you're genuinely sweating. Film sweat dripping off your forearm/elbow onto the asphalt, close as your phone can focus. *Ground-level-ish, fill the frame with the arm + drops, 15s.*

**B — HEAT HAZE + WATCH 🌡️** — Find a long empty stretch of road. Film the shimmer/haze low over the asphalt with sun flare. Then wrist close-up mid-run showing a SLOW pace (7:00+/km). *Haze: crouch low, zoom slightly. Watch: 30cm, 10s each.*

**C — NOON STRUGGLE (POV/BACK) 🥵** — Run real-time under the sun, drenched shirt. Phone at chest pointing down at legs + your shadow directly below you, OR propped filming you from behind, shoulders-down. *15s.*

**D — WATER POUR 🚿** — Stand with your BACK to the camera, sun in front of you. Pour the bottle over the back of your head/neck slowly — spray against the light looks incredible. *Camera 1m behind you, chest height, very still, 2 takes.*

**E — POWER STRIDES SILHOUETTE ☀️** — Run toward/past the camera with the sun directly behind you so you're a dark silhouette, strong strides, drenched. *Propped or gimbal, you run through frame 3–4 times.*

## SESSION 2 — EARLY MORNING (any day)

**F — COOL PAYOFF SHOT 🌅** — Blue hour, crisp air. Smooth FAST relaxed strides, neck-down or silhouette. This must FEEL opposite to A–E: easy, cold, flying. *Gimbal glide or run-throughs, 15s.*

---

**❌ FACE RULE:** face enters frame = reshoot or angle down. Back of head is OK, face never.
**⚠️ HEAT RULE:** you're filming at noon — hydrate for real, keep the shoot under 30 min, film near shade.
**✅ DONE WHEN:** 6 shots × 2 good takes. Session 1 ≈ 25–30 min, Session 2 ≈ 10 min.
