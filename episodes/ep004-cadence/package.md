# EP004 — "Free Speed: The Cadence Fix" (Overstriding / Step Rate)

## 🔬 1. Science / Experiment Anchor
- **Fact:** Heiderscheit et al. (2011): raising step rate by 5–10% (same speed) sharply cuts the energy absorbed by the knee and hip — overstriding (foot landing ahead of the body) is a literal braking force on every step. The "180 steps magic number" is a myth — there's no universal number; the evidence is for +5–10% above YOUR OWN cadence.
- **Pinoy Hook:** Free speed and free injury protection — nothing to buy. Just counting and a free metronome app. Perfect for the runner who can't afford anything.
