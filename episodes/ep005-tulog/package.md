# EP005 — "The Strongest 'Drug' for Your Running: Sleep" (Sleep & Recovery)

## 🔬 1. Science / Experiment Anchor
- **Fact:** Milewski et al. (2014, adolescent athletes): those sleeping under 8 hours were ~1.7x more likely to get injured. Sleep-extension studies on athletes (e.g. Mah et al., 2011) showed faster sprint times and reaction times when sleep was extended. Mechanism: deep sleep releases growth hormone — that's when muscles are repaired; sleep deprivation lowers time-to-exhaustion and slows glycogen replenishment.
- **Pinoy Hook:** The stay-up-late culture — night shift, OT, doom-scroll until 1 AM, then a 4:45 AM run. The strongest performance enhancer is free, legal, and you deny it to yourself every night.
