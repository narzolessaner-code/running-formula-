## 🔬 1. Science / Experiment Anchor

## 🎬 2. Visual Storyboard
1. **Hook (0–3s):** Screenshot ng napiling comment (pangalan naka-blur maliban sa unang pangalan/initial), zoom papasok.
2. **The stats (3–8s):** Monospace na "LAB FILE" card: `PACE: [X]/KM · [Y] KM/WEEK · PROBLEMA: [Z]`.
3. **The reenactment (8–18s):** Ikaw, tumatakbo sa EXACT profile nila — watch close-up sa pace nila, POV, neck-down struggle o ginhawa.
4. **The diagnosis (18–28s):** Slow-mo canvas (foot strike / watch) habang ini-explain ang nakita.
5. **The prescription (28–38s):** POV ng inayos na bersyon (mas mabagal/mas mabilis na hakbang/etc.) — kita ang pagkakaiba.
6. **Casting call ulit (38–45s):** Hero shot + "ikaw ang susunod."
