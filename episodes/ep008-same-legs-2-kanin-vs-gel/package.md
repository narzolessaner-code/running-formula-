## 🔬 1. Science / Experiment Anchor
- **Fact:** Sports nutrition science: a pre-run carbohydrate meal 1–3 hours before running (like rice) raises glycogen and endurance performance. Energy gels are designed for DURING a long run (fast absorption), not as a replacement for real food before running. Carbs are carbs — the difference is timing, amount, and price.
- **Pinoy Hook:** ₱120–₱180 per gel. Rice? Already on your table. "Rice is the original Filipino energy gel." Then we test if that's true — same legs, same road.

## 🧪 THE PROTOCOL
- Same route (5km), same time, 1–2 days apart, same sleep.
- Run A: 1.5 cups of rice (with a little viand) 2 hours before running. Run B: 1 energy gel 15 min before running (nothing else eaten in that window).
- Effort constant: talk test. Record: avg pace, avg HR, hunger/energy feel 1–10 at km 4.
