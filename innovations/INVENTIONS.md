# 🧪 TAKBO LAB — Innovation Lab Registry

Ranked by impact ÷ effort. Status legend: 💡 parked → 🔛 active → ✅ proven → ❌ retired

## 🥇 1. "I TESTED YOUR PACE" — 🔛 activate now
Viewer comments their stats (pace, HR, weekly km, problem); one profile per
week gets run on the creator's own body for a week → faceless reenactment +
coach verdict. Every comment becomes a lottery ticket; comment trap becomes a
casting call; infinite topic supply.
- Cost: lowest — normal shoot + 10 min comment mining.
- First test: end Episode 3 with "Comment your pace, weekly km, and your
  problem. One of you, I run your life next week."

## 🥈 2. "SAME LEGS, DIFFERENT RULES" — 🔛 Episode 3 format
Faceless legs = perfect A/B constant. Same route, same effort, ONE variable
changed (shoes, food, sleep, time of day). Split-screen edit, data overlay
per side, the watch decides. A signature visual format that faced creators
can't copy.
- Cost: medium — run the same route twice, phone propped in the EXACT same
  spot (chalk mark / reference photo).
- First test: EP003 "CARBON PLATE vs ₱1,500 SHOES: Same Legs, Same Road,
  Same Effort."

## 🥉 3. "THE NATION EXPERIMENT" — 💡 parked until ~10 eps / 5k followers
Crowd-sourced science: announce a 14-day zero-gadget protocol (talk-test
running), audience comments baselines, reveal episode shows creator data +
aggregated audience data ("217 runners joined, 73% ran farther before gasping").
3-video arc: announcement → mid-point → reveal. Channel becomes a movement;
quarterly cadence; future brand-deal asset.
- Cost: highest — 14-day arc + manual comment tallying.
