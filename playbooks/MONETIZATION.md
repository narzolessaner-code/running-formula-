# 💰 TAKBO LAB — Monetization Playbook

## Phase 1: 0–10k followers — DON'T MONETIZE OBVIOUSLY YET
- Credibility is the product. No hard sell in the video. The only money moves:
  - Shopee/Lazada affiliate links in the PINNED COMMENTS only (watch, shoes, bottle,
    electrolytes) — "here's the gear I use" style, balanced reviews.
  - Log which pinned link gets clicks (Shopee affiliate dashboard).
- Milestone requirements to target:
  - FB Content Monetization: 5k followers + 60k minutes watch time (90 days)
  - TikTok Creator Rewards: 10k followers + 100k views (30 days), 1+ min videos
    → make the experiment episodes 60–75s as you near 10k.

## Phase 2: 10k–50k — AFFILIATE ENGINE
- Every episode has a natural affiliate slot (Section 7 of the package).
- Make a "LAB GEAR" highlight/guide post — all the links in one place.
- Same Legs episodes = the strongest affiliate content (both shoes linked,
  whoever wins).
- Start the media kit: `brand/` + EPISODE-LOG numbers = deck.

## Phase 3: 50k+ — BRAND DEALS
- Target: hydration brands, sports drinks, insoles, running apparel PH, race
  organizers (sponsor "The Nation Experiment" — gold: an audience that
  follows protocols).
- Pitch template: "Faceless running-science channel, X followers, Y avg views,
  an audience that comments their own training data. We don't endorse — we
  test. Your product goes into a Same Legs episode."
- RULE: the verdict is never for sale. If it's a MYTH, we say MYTH — that's
  exactly why the placement is worth so much.

## Always honest
- One affiliate ask per video, pinned comment only, never in the VO.
- Disclose sponsored content ("This episode has a sponsor, but the result is
  ours").
- The channel that tells the truth about a ₱12,000 shoe is the channel people
  trust at checkout.
