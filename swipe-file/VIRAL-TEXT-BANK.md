# VIRAL TEXT BANK — Ready-to-Use On-Screen Text + Captions

Deadpan, pseudo-scientific runner-brain cope. Same DNA as *"if you slam enough
espresso to hit 125–150 bpm you're basically biohacking zone 2 cardio / I'm
basically running 120 miles per week."*

**How to use:** grab an `OVERLAY` line, put it on your neck-down running clip,
drop the `CAPTION` under it. Overlay = small lie, caption = bigger lie. Keep the
face straight. Tags show who each one hits hardest.

> ⚠️ Rules baked in: punch at our own delusion, never at a person. Never joke
> away real safety (chest pain, heat, hydration). Overlay ≤ 22 words.

---

## THE 8 FORMULAS (the engine — mix these forever)

1. **Cope Equivalence** — "[lazy thing] is basically [elite training thing]."
2. **Delusional Flex** — caption claims elite status from tiny effort.
3. **Fake Study** — "New study confirms [flattering absurd thing]."
4. **Delulu Runner Math** — distance/effort inflation logic.
5. **Nobody: / Runners:** — the in-group callout.
6. **Gear Placebo Science** — new gear gives fake %.
7. **Recovery Cope** — justify rest/food/lying on the floor.
8. **Rationalized Skip** — science-y excuse not to run.
